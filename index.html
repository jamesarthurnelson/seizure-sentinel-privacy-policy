Seizure Sentinel — Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 29, 2026

Seizure Sentinel is a wellness app that summarizes trends in your Apple Watch health data. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, predict, or prevent seizures. This page explains what data the app uses, where it lives, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

Data the app reads

With your permission, the app reads the following from Apple Health, sourced only from your Apple Watch:

  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Wrist temperature (sleeping)
  • Respiratory rate
  • Sleep analysis
  • Step count

With your permission, the app also uses your device's location, only to automatically detect travel and altitude changes that can affect the patterns above. Location is never required to use the app's core features.

Data you enter yourself

You may optionally log seizure dates (and an optional note) directly in the app, to track your own history and let the app show you its own accuracy against that history.

Where this data lives

All of the data described above stays on your device. It is stored in a local database inside the app and is never uploaded, transmitted, sold, or shared with us or with any third party — including us. We do not operate any server that receives your health data, your location, or your seizure log.

What the app downloads

The app periodically downloads a small set of anonymous, non-personal configuration values (the numeric thresholds its scoring uses) from a read-only public database. This download contains no information about you and is not tied to your identity, Apple ID, or device in any way we can access.

Anonymized outcome reporting

The app contains groundwork for an anonymized process that could someday help validate and improve its scoring model, by comparing risk-level changes against seizures you've logged. If this is ever active, it is designed so that:

  • Reports are linked only by a random identifier generated on your device — never your Apple ID, iCloud account, or any other identifier tied to you personally.
  • Reports contain only category labels and day-resolution timing — never exact values, exact timestamps, your location, or free text you've written.

As of this version of the app, there is no active destination for these reports. They are not transmitted anywhere. If a real destination is ever enabled, this policy will be updated first, and you will be asked to acknowledge the change before it takes effect.

What we don't do

Seizure Sentinel contains no advertising, no third-party analytics software, and no data brokers. We do not sell data, because we do not receive any to sell.

Your control over this data

Since everything is stored locally, deleting the app deletes all of its data with it. There is no account to close and no remote copy to request deletion of, because none exists.

Children's privacy

Seizure Sentinel is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data leaves your device, the app will ask you to review and acknowledge the change before it takes effect.
