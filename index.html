Seizure Sentinel Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026. Matches the in-app consent version 2026-11-07.1.

Seizure Sentinel is a health diary for people with and without seizures. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, predict, prevent or treat seizures. This page explains what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can download or delete it.

Joining means sharing

When you join, your data uploads to your Sentinel account so the app can show you your record, compare each day with your own history and, as more people join, with the community. Sentinel is for adults (18 and over).

What Sentinel collects

From Apple Health, with your permission:

  • Heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate
  • Breathing rate and wrist temperature
  • Sleep, steps and falls
  • Period days: the dates only, never flow or other cycle data
  • Date of birth and sex: used to fill in the questions; only your age range and sex are stored, never your birth date

From your Apple Watch: movement readings from its accelerometer, recorded in the background, with which wrist the Watch is on and which side its crown faces.

From your iPhone: how the phone moves while Sentinel is open, to measure how steady your hand is. Nothing is recorded while the app is closed.

What you enter:

  • Diary entries for seizures and other symptoms: the type, date and time, any tags you tap (for example Stressed or Missed medication), and any note you write
  • Medication changes (the date, never the medicine's name unless you write it in a note) and day notes
  • Your answers to the four-weekly seizure check-in
  • Your answers when you join: whether you have a seizure diagnosis, age range, sex, country (and US region), dominant hand and, optionally, seizure type
  • Whether you'd like to be contacted about future research (off unless you turn it on)

Time zones: the hours your phone was ahead of or behind UTC on each day, and trips as dates and hours from home. Never the name of a time zone or a place. Sentinel doesn't use your location.

Your consent: each version of the consent screen you accept, with its date.

How it is used

  • To show you your own data: Today, your Record, Patterns and the physician summary you can make.
  • To compare your days with your own history, and with community totals that never identify anyone.
  • To check the app is working well (for example, how complete uploads are).

Your notes are kept only so your diary comes back if you change phones. They are not analysed.

Where it is stored

On Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, encrypted, under a random ID created on your phone. Sign in with Apple only confirms that uploads come from you: Sentinel stores a one-way scrambled form of your Apple account identifier to check that, and never sees your name or email. Your diary is not stored in iCloud.

What we don't do

Sentinel doesn't sell your data, doesn't use it for advertising, and doesn't share it with any third party. There is no advertising or third-party analytics software in the app. AWS stores the data on our behalf, and Apple provides Sign in with Apple.

Your control

  • Download: Settings → Your data makes one file with everything Sentinel stores for you.
  • Delete: Settings → Your data → Delete removes everything Sentinel stores for you, on its servers and on your phone, and ends Sentinel's Sign in with Apple access. This can't be undone. Your Apple Health data isn't touched.
  • New phone: sign in with Apple to bring your diary back.
  • Permissions: you can turn off any Health category, motion access or notifications in iOS Settings at any time.

Data is kept until you delete it.

Children

Sentinel is for adults. Anyone who answers that they're under 18 can't join, and nothing they enter is uploaded.

Changes to this policy

If what Sentinel collects changes, the app shows the consent screen again and asks you to accept the new version before it takes effect, and this page is updated.
