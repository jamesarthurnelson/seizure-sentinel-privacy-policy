Seizure Sentinel Support

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Seizure Sentinel is a data-driven health diary. For help, questions or to report a problem, email jamesarthurnelson@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone and Apple Watch models and what you were doing when the problem happened.

In an emergency, don't email. Follow your seizure action plan and call your local emergency number.

Common questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No, you can log without one. The readings (sleep, heart rate, HRV and the rest) come from an Apple Watch through Apple Health, so without one those screens wait for data.

Why are some readings missing?

Sentinel can only show what Apple Health has. Check that the Watch was worn, that Health access is on for each category (in the Health app, tap your picture, then Apps, then Seizure Sentinel), and open Sentinel once so it can catch up. Settings → Activity in the app shows its last upload and how recent your Watch data is.

Does Sentinel detect or predict seizures?

No. It's a diary, not a medical device. It shows your readings around the entries you log, compared with your own history. Check with your doctor before any medical decision.

I have a new phone. How do I get my diary back?

Install Sentinel, tap Join and sign in with the same Apple account. Sentinel finds your diary and offers to restore it.

How do I download or delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Your data. Download makes one file with everything Sentinel stores for you. Delete removes everything from Sentinel's servers and your phone and can't be undone; your Apple Health data isn't touched.

Who can use Sentinel?

Adults 18 and over, with or without seizures: patients, partners, parents, caregivers, and anyone curious about their own health.
